# PIC action brief

The Action brief PDF uses four sections: Your assignment (PIC, decision owner, completion date, review date and status); agreed actions (what/how in wide columns); success measures (measure, target, evidence); and report-back checklist (actual result, evidence, blockers/support and next action). Background and earlier experiment results follow the action sections. Full case exports retain the existing worksheet, conversations and history.

The brief is generated from saved records without an extra AI call or credit charge. It does not invent tasks, deadlines, names, targets or results. Generic role placeholders are explicitly marked as needing a name. Missing values remain to agree. An overall target identical to a KPI target is shown once. Earlier experiment results are separate from execution KPI results; unconfirmed outcomes stay labelled. Conditional rollout drafts remain explicitly unapproved. Long saved text flows onto extra pages rather than being silently truncated.

A brief supports accountability through a named PIC, named decision owner, due date, measurable target, evidence source and review date. It does not send reminders, obtain acknowledgements, or automatically verify results. Users must agree missing details and record progress in SOLVE OS.
